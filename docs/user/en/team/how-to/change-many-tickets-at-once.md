---
title: Change many tickets at once
description: Change the status, the priority or the assignee of up to 100 tickets of the list in one step.
---

1. Select **Tickets** in the main navigation. Choose a view or select **Add filter** to list the tickets you want to change ([ticket list](../reference/ticket-list.md)).
2. Select the checkbox of each ticket – on a phone, the checkbox on its card. The checkbox in the heading of the table selects every loaded ticket; select **Show more** first to load more. You can select at most 100 tickets.
3. In the bar that shows how many tickets are selected, choose the change:
   - **Status**, then the new status. For **Rejected**, enter the **Reason**: it is used for every selected ticket and added to each of them as your public comment. **Duplicate** is not offered.
   - **Priority**, then the new priority.
   - **Assignee**, then **Assign to me** or **Unassigned**. When all selected tickets are in one project and you can assign people there, you can also choose one of the people who can be assigned in it.
4. Check the result:
   - When every ticket was changed, a message says so, for example "20 tickets updated".
   - Otherwise a window lists each ticket that was not changed with its reason, for example "Changed by someone else – reload to see the latest version". These tickets stay selected: select **Reload and retry** to load the list again and repeat the change on them.

Select **Clear selection** to deselect every ticket. Changing the view, a filter or the order clears the selection too.

Each ticket is changed on its own, as if you had changed it in the [ticket view](../reference/ticket-view.md), and the change appears in its **Activity**. There is no **Undo** for a bulk change: to change tickets back, select them and choose their previous value.
