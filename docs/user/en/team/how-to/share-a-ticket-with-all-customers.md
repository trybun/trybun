---
title: Share a ticket with all customers
description: Approve or refuse a customer's request to share a ticket, or change the visibility of a ticket yourself.
---

Customers choose who sees their tickets: **Only me**, **My organization** or **All customers**. A ticket becomes visible to all customers only after one person of the team approves it. Every Developer, Project manager and the Administrator of the project can decide.

## Decide a sharing request

A ticket whose author asks to share it shows **Sharing requested** under **Visibility** in its fields.

1. Open the ticket and read the description, the files and the title: once shared, every customer of the project sees them.
2. To share it, select **Share with all customers** and confirm with **Share with all customers** after checking that nothing identifies the customer.
3. To refuse, select **Refuse**, enter the **Reason** and select **Refuse**. The ticket keeps its visibility, and the author sees that the team did not share it. The reason is added to the timeline as your public comment, which the author sees ([comments and internal notes](../reference/comments-and-internal-notes.md)).

The first decision counts. If a colleague decided first, Trybun says "This sharing request has already been decided."

## Change the visibility yourself

In **Visibility**, choose the new value. **Only me** and **My organization** are saved at once; **All customers** asks for the same confirmation as an approval. You can share a ticket only when its author shares it with their organization – a ticket visible to **Only me** is shared only on its author's request. Decide a waiting request before changing the visibility.

Tickets created by the team or by integrations are visible to the team only and have no **Visibility** field.

Every change and decision appears in the **Activity** of the ticket and in the audit log.
