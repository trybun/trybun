---
title: Take a product tour again
description: Start the short guided tour of a view again from the help menu.
---

A product tour shows you around a view in a few short steps. It starts by itself the first time you open a view that has one – once for your account, on whichever device you use first.

1. Select the help icon (question mark) in the header.
2. Select **Take the tour** followed by the name of the tour.

The item appears only on views that have a tour. Move through the steps with **Next** and **Back**, and finish with **Done**.

## Close a tour

Press **Esc**, or select the close button (×) or **Skip tour**; on a phone you can also swipe the tip off the screen. A tour you close, skip or leave by opening another view does not start by itself again – take it again from the help menu whenever you like. When a tour changes noticeably, it starts by itself once more.
