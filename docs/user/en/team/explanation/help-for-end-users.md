---
title: What end users see in the help
description: How the help is divided between end users, the team and the Administrator.
---

The help of Trybun has pages for three audiences:

- **End users** – how to report and follow tickets.
- **Team** – how to handle tickets and plan work.
- **Administrator** – how to configure Trybun.

End users receive only the pages for end users: the team and Administrator pages are not shown to them, are not found by their search and cannot be opened by their address. Team members receive the pages of every audience.

When you point a customer to the help, send a link to a page for end users. Its address is the same in every language, and the page appears in the language the customer uses in Trybun.
