Trybun is where the team handles the tickets of customers and its own work. This tutorial shows how to sign in and what you see after signing in.

## Sign in

1. Open the address of Trybun in your browser. The sign-in screen appears.
2. Select **GitHub** and sign in with your GitHub account.
3. Enter the one-time code from your authenticator app. Two-factor authentication is required for every team member; on your first sign-in Trybun asks you to set up the authenticator app.

After signing in you see the **Home** page.

## Find your way around

- The **header** at the top shows the Trybun logo, which always takes you back to **Home**. On its right are the help icon and your account menu with the language switch and **Sign out**.
- The **main navigation** lists the views of Trybun. On a phone it is at the bottom of the screen; on a tablet or a computer it is on the left.

## Get help

The help icon (question mark) in the header opens the help page about the current view. The team sees the pages for end users too, so you know what your customers read; [what end users see in the help](../explanation/help-for-end-users.md) explains the difference.
