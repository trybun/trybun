Trybun is where the team handles the tickets of customers and its own work. This tutorial shows how to sign in and what you see after signing in.

## Sign in

1. Open the address of Trybun in your browser. The sign-in screen appears.
2. Select **GitHub** and sign in with your GitHub account.
3. Enter the one-time code from your authenticator app. Two-factor authentication is required for every team member; on your first sign-in Trybun asks you to set up the authenticator app.

After signing in you see the **Home** page, and a short tour shows you around it. Follow it with **Next**, or close it with **Esc** – you can [take it again](../how-to/take-a-product-tour-again.md) at any time.

To protect the data of your customers, Trybun signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, and 12 hours after you signed in even if you keep working. Sign in again with your GitHub account and a new one-time code.

## Find your way around

- The **header** at the top shows the Trybun logo, which always takes you back to **Home**. On its right are the help menu (question mark) and your account menu with the language switch and **Sign out**.
- The **main navigation** lists the views of Trybun. On a phone it is at the bottom of the screen; on a tablet or a computer it is on the left.

## Get help

The help menu (question mark) in the header opens the help page about the current view with **Help for this view**, and starts the tour of the view again. The team sees the pages for end users too, so you know what your customers read; [what end users see in the help](../explanation/help-for-end-users.md) explains the difference.
