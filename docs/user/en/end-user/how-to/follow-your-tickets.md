## See your tickets

Select **My tickets** in the main navigation. It lists the tickets you reported, in all your projects. The home of a project shows your latest tickets of that project; **Show all** opens the whole list of the project.

Tickets marked **Awaiting your reply** come first: the team needs something from you – more information, or the confirmation that your problem is solved. The others follow, the most recently updated first.

## Follow one ticket

Select a ticket to open it. It shows:

- its status and what it means at the moment, for example **In progress** – "We are testing the solution";
- your description;
- its history: when you created it and every change of its status, with who made it.

When the team asks whether your problem is solved, the ticket shows the question at the top: see [Confirm that your problem is solved](confirm-that-your-problem-is-solved.md).
