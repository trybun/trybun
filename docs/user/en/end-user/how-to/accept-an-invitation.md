---
title: Accept an invitation
description: Open your invitation link, create your account with a password or sign in with Microsoft, Google or GitHub, and start using Trybun.
views: ['/invitations']
---

You received a link to Trybun from the person who invited you. The link works once and is valid for 7 days.

1. Open the whole link in your browser. **Accept the invitation** shows your e-mail address, what you are invited to and who invited you. Your account always uses this address.
2. Accept it in one of the ways below. Afterwards Trybun opens its home page, and you have access to what you were invited to.

## With a password

1. Under **Create your account**, enter your **First name**, **Last name** and a **Password** ([choose a password](choose-a-password.md)).
2. Select **Accept and sign in**.
3. The sign-in screen appears with your address filled in. Enter the password you just chose.

## With Microsoft or Google

When they are offered, select **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Google** and sign in there. Your Microsoft or Google address may differ from the invited one; your Trybun account keeps the invited address.

## As a team member, with GitHub

Team members always sign in with GitHub and a one-time code from an authenticator app.

1. Select **Continue with GitHub** and sign in to GitHub. Your GitHub address may differ from the invited one.
2. Trybun signs you in once more and asks you to set up two-factor authentication: scan the QR code with an authenticator app and enter its code.
3. When asked, sign in once more with a one-time code from the app. Then the invitation is accepted.

## If you already have an account

If you already have a Trybun account with the invited address, select **Sign in to accept** and sign in as usual. If you are signed in with another account, sign out and open the link again.

## If the link does not work

**This invitation link is not valid** means the link has expired, has been used, or was replaced by a new one. Ask the person who invited you for a new link.
