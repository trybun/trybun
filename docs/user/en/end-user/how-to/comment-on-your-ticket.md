title: Comment on your ticket
description: Reply to the team, change your title and description, and edit or delete your comments.

## Reply to the team

1. Open the ticket from **My tickets**.
2. Write your reply in the comment field below the history of the ticket. You can format the text ([format text](../reference/formatting-text.md)) and attach files ([attach files](attach-files.md)).
3. Send it.

Your comment is visible to the team and to the customers who see the ticket now ([choose who can see your ticket](choose-who-can-see-your-ticket.md)). If you later share the ticket more widely, the comment stays visible only to those who could see it when you wrote it.

## What your reply does to the ticket

| The ticket is | After your reply |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting your reply** – the team asked whether your problem is solved | The team continues working on it, as with **No, still a problem** ([confirm that your problem is solved](confirm-that-your-problem-is-solved.md)) |
| **Awaiting your reply** – the team needs more information | The team continues working on it |
| **Resolved** less than 14 days ago | The ticket is reopened |
| Any other status | The status does not change |

A ticket that is closed – rejected, merged with another one, or resolved 14 days ago or more – takes no new comments. It shows **Create a new ticket** instead: report the problem again in a new ticket ([report a problem](report-a-problem.md)).

## Mention people

Type `@` and choose a person from the list. You can mention the team members who take part in the ticket – the person working on it and those who replied to you – and, when the ticket is visible to your organization or to all customers, the people of your organization. A mention does not give anyone access to the ticket.

## Change your title and description

Select **Edit** next to the title or the description of a ticket you reported, change the text and save it.

## Edit or delete your comment

Open the **⋯** menu of your comment:

- **Edit** changes the text, also on a closed ticket.
- **Delete** removes the comment with its files after a confirmation. This cannot be undone.

A changed comment or description shows **edited**. For your own texts, select **edited** to see the earlier versions; for the texts of other people you see only the marker.
