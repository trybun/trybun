---
title: Choose who can see your ticket
description: Keep a ticket to yourself, share it with your organization, or ask the team to share it with all customers.
---

Every ticket starts as **Only me**: only you and the team see it. You can choose another visibility when you create the ticket, in **Visibility** below **Description**, or later in the ticket itself.

| Visibility | Who sees the ticket |
|---|---|
| **Only me** | You and the team |
| **My organization** | Also the people of your organization who work in the same project. Offered only when your organization has access to the project |
| **All customers** | Every customer of the project, after the team approves your request |

## Share a ticket with your organization

1. Open the ticket from **My tickets**.
2. In **Visibility**, select **My organization**.

A message confirms the change. To make the ticket private again, select **Only me**.

## Ask to share a ticket with all customers

1. Open the ticket, or create a new one.
2. In **Visibility**, select **All customers**. Read the note "Make sure the description and attachments contain no confidential data." – everything in the ticket becomes visible to other companies.

The ticket shows "Waiting for the team to share this ticket with all customers." Until then, your organization sees it, if it has access to the project; otherwise only you do. When the team approves, the history of the ticket shows **Shared with everyone**.

To withdraw a request that is still waiting, select **Only me** or **My organization**.

If the team does not share the ticket, it says "The team did not share this ticket with all customers." The team's reason is a comment in the history of the ticket. The ticket keeps its visibility, and you can ask again.

## What you cannot change

A ticket shared with all customers can be narrowed only by the team. People of other organizations see you as **Customer**, not by your name.

If you leave your organization, you keep your own tickets that are visible to **Only me** or **All customers**; tickets you shared with your old organization stay with it.
