---
title: Choose a password
description: The rules for a password in Trybun and how to choose one that is strong and easy to remember.
---

You choose a password when you create your account from an invitation and when you reset a forgotten one. Trybun accepts a password that:

- has **at least 15 and at most 64 characters** – spaces and any letters count;
- is **not your e-mail address**;
- is **not a common password** – Trybun refuses passwords that are known from lists of leaked passwords.

There are no rules about capital letters, digits or special characters, and you never have to change a password just because time has passed: length protects a password best.

## A strong password that is easy to remember

- Use a **phrase of several words** that belong together only for you, e.g. `kettle on the balcony sings at dawn` – but not this one, which is now public.
- Or let a **password manager** generate and remember the password for you.
- Use the password **only for Trybun**: a password reused on another site is exposed when that site leaks.

If the sign-in screen refuses a password, its message says which rule it breaks; choose a longer or less common one.
