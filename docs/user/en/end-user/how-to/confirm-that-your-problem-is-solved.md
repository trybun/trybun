---
title: Confirm that your problem is solved
description: Tell the team with one press whether the solution works for you.
views: ['/portal/tickets/[identifier]/resolution-confirmation']
---

When the team has solved your problem, it may ask you to confirm it. The ticket is then **Awaiting your reply** and shows the question **Is your problem solved?** at the top.

1. Open the ticket from **My tickets**.
2. Select **Yes, solved** or **No, still a problem**.

With **Yes, solved** the ticket is resolved. With **No, still a problem** the team continues working on it. Your answer appears in the history of the ticket.

If you do not answer within 7 days, Trybun considers the problem solved.

If the team or Trybun has already closed the ticket, a message says that your answer is no longer needed.
