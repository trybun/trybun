## Change the language

1. Select your account menu (your initials) in the header.
2. Under **Language**, select the language you want.

The page you are on appears again in the chosen language. Trybun saves the choice in your account, so it applies on every device you sign in with.

## Change the time zone

1. Select your account menu (your initials) in the header, then **Account settings**.
2. Under **Time zone**, search for your time zone and select it, or select **Use this device's time zone**.
3. Select **Save**.

Dates and times appear in this time zone on every device. Until you choose one, Trybun uses the default time zone of the system. You can change the language on this page too.
