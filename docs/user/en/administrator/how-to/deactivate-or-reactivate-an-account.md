---
title: Deactivate or reactivate an account
description: Stop a person from signing in at once, give the access back later, and grant or revoke the Administrator role.
---

Deactivate the account of a person who should no longer use Trybun, for example when they leave the company. Accounts are never deleted this way: their tickets, comments and history stay as they are.

## Deactivate an account

1. Open **Administration**, then **Accounts**.
2. Find the person by name or e-mail address.
3. Open the actions of their account (**⋮**) and select **Deactivate**.
4. Confirm with **Deactivate**.

At once, the person is signed out on every device and cannot sign in again; their pending invitations are revoked. Their access to projects and organizations is kept for a later reactivation.

## Reactivate an account

Open the actions of a deactivated account and select **Reactivate**. The person can sign in again with the access they had. Revoked invitations stay revoked: an account that never accepted an invitation returns to **Invited** and needs a new invitation.

## Grant or revoke the Administrator role

Open the actions of a team member's account and select **Grant Administrator role** or **Revoke Administrator role** (confirm with **Revoke role**). Only team members can be Administrators.

## What you cannot do

- Deactivate your own account or revoke your own Administrator role.
- Deactivate or demote the last active Administrator – Trybun must always have one.
