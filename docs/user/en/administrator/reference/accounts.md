**Administration** › **Accounts** lists every account: team members and end users, including people who have been invited but have not accepted yet. On a phone each account is a card.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The display name; **No name yet** until the person accepts an invitation |
| **E-mail address** | The address the account uses; it never changes by signing in with GitHub, Microsoft or Google |
| **Kind** | **Team** – signs in with GitHub and a one-time code; **End user** – signs in with a password, Microsoft or Google |
| **State** | See below; **Administrator** marks the Administrators |

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Invited** | The account was created by an invitation that has not been accepted yet |
| **Active** | The person can sign in |
| **Deactivated** | The person cannot sign in ([deactivate or reactivate an account](../how-to/deactivate-or-reactivate-an-account.md)) |
| **Anonymized** | The personal data of the account was removed |

## Filters and search

Filter by **Kind** and **State**, and search by name or e-mail address. The filters are part of the address of the page, so you can bookmark or share a filtered list.

## Actions

The actions of an account (**⋮**) offer only what is possible for it: **Deactivate**, **Reactivate**, **Grant Administrator role** and **Revoke Administrator role**. Your own account has no actions.

**Invite** invites a person to a project, an organization or the Administrator role; the pending invitations of an account offer **Copy new link** and **Revoke**, as on the Members tab of a project ([invite people to a project](../../team/how-to/invite-people-to-a-project.md)).
